# Whats-Up

Privacy policy and "Delete your data" pages for What's Up, a free Fire TV app by Spacely Digital LLC.

Published with GitHub Pages: https://spacelydigital.github.io/Whats-Up/

- `privacy/` the privacy policy (the same words as the app's Privacy page)
- `delete/` how to delete your data

Plain HTML: no scripts, fonts, cookies or analytics.
