Delete your data

What's Up for Fire TV, by Spacely Digital LLC

What's Up keeps everything on your TV. We have no server and no copy of your location, settings or history, and there are no accounts, so there is nothing to delete on our side except emails and tip records (below).

Delete what the app stored on your TV

Either way deletes everything at once:

  1. In the app: open Settings › Privacy › Erase all data, and confirm. The app returns to its first screen and sends nothing until you agree again.
  2. Without opening the app: on Fire TV, go to Settings › Applications › Manage Installed Applications, pick What's Up, and choose Uninstall. Menu names vary by model. This works even if the app won't open.

Moving your location in the app by more than about 2 miles (3 km) also clears today's log and your recent sightings.

Delete what we hold

We only hold emails you send us and records of tips sent through Ko-fi. To see them or have them deleted, email spacelydigital@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We'll reply within 45 days, usually much sooner, and there is no charge. We keep only the tip records the law requires for tax purposes, for 7 years.

Logs kept by other services

The public services the app talks to keep their own short logs, which we can't see or delete. The privacy policy lists each one, with a link to its own policy.
